Our story

Jerry Luxury started on a single sewing machine on a quiet street off Airport Road, built on the belief that Benin City deserved fashion that felt as considered as anything coming out of Lagos or Abuja.

Today we're a full design studio and atelier — pattern cutters, embroiderers and finishers working together on every commission, from a single agbada to matching outfits for an entire groom's party. We still measure every client by hand, because a garment that fits properly changes how a person carries themselves.

We work in Ankara, aso-oke, lace, adire and fine wools, pairing traditional Edo and Yoruba textile craft with tailoring built for how people actually move, sit and dance.

How a commission works

Five steps, from first call to final fitting.

01

Consultation

We talk through occasion, fabric and budget, in-store or on a WhatsApp call.

02

Measurement

A full hand-measurement session at our Sakponba Road atelier, or a guided self-measurement kit for clients outside Benin City.

03

Design & cut

Pattern drafted and fabric cut, with a sketch shared for approval before stitching begins.

04

Fitting

One or two fitting sessions to adjust drape, length and closures before final finishing.
